Day-by-day risk for being outside, driving and flying

Trip plan

Forecasts reach about 16 days ahead. Days further out show when their forecast opens.

Departure time is local to the first stop. Points are spaced along the route and each is checked at the time you would pass it.

Planning guidance only. These cards are distilled from computer model forecasts and simple rules of thumb. They are not an official weather briefing. Before a flight, get a full briefing from an approved source such as 1800wxbrief.com or ForeFlight, and check NOTAMs and TFRs.

Ceilings and flight categories are estimated from model cloud layers and humidity, not observed. Airport codes work as places, but check the airport's own METAR and TAF too. Weather data: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), road routes from OSRM, place names from BigDataCloud.
